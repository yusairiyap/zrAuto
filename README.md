## 🔥 The big ones

|   | Feature | Why it stands out |
|:-:|---|---|
| 📥 | **YouTube, offline** | Download videos, playlists or just the audio. They play inside your lists with no signal, downloads resume where they stopped, and a stalled stream flips to the saved copy mid-song without a gap. |
| 🚗 | **Steering-wheel car mode** | Previous / Next move a glowing outline across any screen; hold Next to open, hold Previous to go back. Lists, search, queue, menus and Settings, with your hands on the wheel. |
| 🎤 | **Lyrics that scroll with the song** | Synced lyrics glide over the album cover, and a tap opens them full size right in the Music tab. Tap a line to jump, drag to scrub. Captions over fullscreen video too. |
| 🔋 | **Live power meter** | An Ampere-style gauge of the phone's real battery draw, a strip over your music or video, session min / max, and a daily trend split by video, music and Android Auto. |
| 🍃 | **Power saver** | Dims the screen, holds 60 Hz and pauses downloads: by hand, at a battery level, when Android Auto connects, or with Android's own Battery Saver. |
| 📊 | **Data usage tracker** | See what videos, music mode and downloads cost you, and set a warning or hard limit before the plan runs dry. |
| 🧩 | **All in One card** | One card of big tiles (sleep timer, video quality, power saver, comments and more), opened by a floating button or a wheel key. You choose the tiles and their order. |
| 📲 | **Share over Wi-Fi** | Send songs, playlists, folders, downloaded videos or even the app itself to a phone nearby. The receiver accepts, both see progress. |

<details>
<summary><b>🚗 More on car mode</b></summary>

<br>

* Previous / Next move a glowing outline; hold Next to open or play, hold Previous to go back.
* Works in lists, YouTube search, the Music queue, menus and Settings, only while Android Auto is connected.
* On YouTube and the browser, the outline walks the page's links and buttons.
* The car screen has its own set of floating buttons and All in One tiles.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>📲 More on sharing</b></summary>

<br>

* Pick a song, playlist, favorites or a whole folder, then tap a nearby phone on the same Wi-Fi.
* Downloaded videos and files go along with it.
* The other person always accepts or declines, and you both see the progress with a Cancel button.
* Or send just the YouTube links through any app you like.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>🔋 More on power usage</b></summary>

<br>

* The gauge colours (light, moderate, heavy) are judged against your battery's size, which you can set yourself.
* Time to full or flat is worked from a steady recent average, not the last reading.
* Reach it from a floating button, a key binding, All in One or the Info overlay strip.
* Android Auto sessions are counted apart, since a connected car draws more.

</details>

## 🎼 The player underneath

zrAuto is built on [Andrey Pavlenko's Fermata Auto](https://github.com/AndreyPavlenko/Fermata), and keeps everything that made it a great player:

| | |
|---|---|
| 📁 **Folders, the way you think** | Browse and play your media organized by folders, and zrAuto remembers where you left off in each one. |
| ⭐ **Favorites and playlists** | Save tracks and folders, build playlists, and use CUE and M3U files. |
| 🔖 **Bookmarks** | Mark spots in a track or video to jump back to. |
| 🎛️ **Per-track audio** | Equalizer, Bass / Volume Boost and Virtualizer, plus playback speed, tuned per track or folder. |
| 🔤 **Titles your way** | Customize how titles and subtitles look. |
| 📺 **IPTV** | Live TV with EPG and catch-up. |
| 📱 **Android Auto and Android TV** | Plus your favorites and playlists on the Android TV home screen. |
| ⚙️ **Your choice of engine** | MediaPlayer, ExoPlayer or VLC, whichever suits your files; VLC adds subtitle and audio-track support. |

## ⬇️ Get it

Grab the latest APK from the **[Releases page](https://github.com/yusairiyap/zrAuto/releases/latest)** and install it on your phone. zrAuto tells you in the app whenever a new version is out.

## 🛠️ Build it yourself

<details>
<summary>From source</summary>

<br>

1. Install [Android Studio](https://developer.android.com/studio) (or just the Android SDK).
2. Point `ANDROID_SDK_ROOT` at your SDK folder:
   ```bash
   export ANDROID_SDK_ROOT=<path to your Android SDK>
   ```
3. Clone the project:
   ```bash
   git clone --recurse-submodules https://github.com/yusairiyap/zrAuto.git
   cd zrAuto
   ```
4. Build it:
   ```bash
   ./gradlew bundleAutoRelease
   ```
   The finished app package shows up under the project's build output folders.

</details>
